# What would a solar roof actually save you?

*A free tool that answers that question for any address in Morocco.*

**[atlas-nexus-69o.pages.dev/solar](https://atlas-nexus-69o.pages.dev/solar/)**

---

Morocco has one of the best solar resources in the world. Fewer than
1 % of homes have a panel.

The blocker isn't sunlight or hardware cost. It's that nobody can tell
a homeowner what a roof would actually save them. The people holding
those numbers are installers, and their estimate arrives attached to a
sales call.

So I built a tool that just answers the question.

**Why the numbers work.** ONEE's tariff is progressive: 0,90 MAD/kWh at
the bottom, 1,60 MAD above 500 kWh a month. Solar doesn't displace your
average kilowatt-hour — it displaces your most expensive one, from the
top down. So the bigger your bill, the faster it pays:

- 400 MAD/month → 2 kWc, ~2 375 MAD saved a year, ~10-year payback
- 1 200 MAD/month → 5 kWc, ~7 458 MAD saved a year, ~8-year payback

**How it works.** Type an address, get one number. The only control is
your monthly bill — not kilowatt-hours, because that's what people
actually know. Production comes from PVGIS, the European Commission's
model, at your exact coordinates. Tariffs are ONEE's published grid.
Every assumption is adjustable.

No login, no email, no lead capture. I don't sell panels.

It's an estimate, not a quote — enough to tell you whether the
conversation with an installer is worth having.
