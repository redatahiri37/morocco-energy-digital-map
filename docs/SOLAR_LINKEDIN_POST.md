Morocco gets 1 800–2 200 kWh per kWc per year. Among the best solar
resources on earth.

Fewer than 1 % of Moroccan homes have a panel.

That gap was never about sunlight or hardware cost. It was about
information — nobody could tell a homeowner what a roof would actually
produce, save, and pay back. The only people holding those numbers were
installers, and their estimate came attached to a sales call.

So I built the answer and made it free.

Here is the part most people miss. ONEE's residential tariff is
progressive: 0,90 MAD/kWh at the bottom, 1,60 MAD/kWh above 500 kWh a
month. Solar does not displace your average kilowatt-hour — it displaces
your most expensive one, from the top down.

Which means the bigger your bill, the faster solar pays:

→ 400 MAD/month bill: 2 kWc, ~2 375 MAD saved/year, ~10-year payback
→ 1 200 MAD/month bill: 5 kWc, ~7 458 MAD saved/year, ~8-year payback

And since decree 2.25.100 activated Law 82-21 in March 2026,
self-consumption under 11 kW is a simple declaration.

**Atlas Solar**: type an address, get one number. One control on the
first screen — your monthly bill, not kilowatt-hours, because that is
what people actually know. Production from PVGIS (European Commission).
Tariffs from ONEE. Every assumption exposed and adjustable.

No login. No email. No lead capture. I don't sell panels.

Morocco is exporting the utility-scale solar story to Europe while the
