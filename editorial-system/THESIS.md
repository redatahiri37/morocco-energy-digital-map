# Editorial Thesis — v0 DRAFT (Reda to refine)

> Every Scout signal and Writer draft is evaluated against this thesis.
> If a signal doesn't bend toward or against the thesis, it's not a story.

## Working thesis
**Morocco is the first emerging market where hyperscaler and industrial
electron demand will rewrite the grid plan faster than the grid plan can
be rewritten — and the gap between the two clocks is where the policy,
capital, and geopolitics of the next decade get decided.**

## Three sub-claims the publication defends
1. **The electron's passport matters as much as its carbon intensity.**
   CBAM, green hydrogen certification, and EU-MEA interconnection
   frame Morocco not as a renewable producer but as a
   *qualified supplier* to European industry.
2. **Compute load is the new industrial load.**
   Data center siting in MEA is a grid-planning question, not a
   real-estate question. The incumbents (ONEE, MASEN) are being asked
   to plan for a demand curve they've never seen.
3. **Industrial demand response is the missing flexibility layer.**
   OCP, cement, steel — the phosphate-and-minerals economy — is the
   cheapest battery Morocco has. Nobody is pricing it yet.

## What the publication is NOT
- Not a renewables-cheerleading blog
- Not a consulting deck in prose form
- Not a policy explainer for non-specialists
- Not balanced — it has a view and defends it

## House voice
Technical fluency assumed. Short sentences. Numbers anchor every claim.
One counter-argument per piece, addressed head-on. No "excited to share."
Register: Pawel Czyzak (Ember) + David Fickling (Bloomberg Opinion) +
the early Stratechery analytical frame.
