# Brief: Nexus AI Factory — Nouaceur, Casablanca
**Week:** 2026-W17 (Apr 20–26) / **Scout signal:** raw-signals.md 2026-04-24
**Thesis sub-claim:** primarily #2 (compute load is new industrial load);
secondary #1 (electron's passport); secondary #3 (flexibility gap)

## The number that anchors the piece
**500 MW** — target AI compute load at one site, to be supplied
100% from renewables by TAQA Morocco.
Context: Morocco's 2025 peak demand ≈ 7 GW; this single site at full
build ≈ 7% of national peak. Installed capacity ≈ 12 GW.
*Source: Nexus AI Factory announcement, GITEX Africa Morocco, April 2026.*

## Three evidence points
1. **Consortium and money.** $1.2B total investment. Lead: Nexus Core
   Systems (US). Operator: Naver Cloud (South Korea). Chips: Nvidia
   GB200 Blackwell. Financier: Lloyds Capital. Power: TAQA Morocco.
   *(WebSearch synthesis across Morocco World News, Hespress, Silicon Africa, April 2026)*
2. **Phased build, slipping.** Phase 1 was originally 40 MW for Q1
   2026; now reportedly 16 MW activated with MAD 5B mobilized and 50
   direct jobs at phase 1. Target 500 MW by end of phase 2 in 2027.
   *(Enterprise AM, Morocco World News, April 2026)*
3. **Geographic logic.** Nouaceur sits at the landing of multiple
   submarine fiber cables connecting to Europe — low latency for
   European AI inference workloads. Morocco reportedly chosen over
   South Africa as the site for this consortium.
   *(iAfrica, Silicon Africa, Enterprise AM, April 2026)*

## The counter-argument
Three honest doubts:
- **Announcement vs. commissioning.** Nvidia-partnered data center
  announcements have run ahead of actual GW on the ground globally.
  The Nvidia delegation visited Morocco in November 2025 — the chip
  allocation may be conditional on phase completion, not guaranteed.
- **"100% renewable" means what.** TAQA Morocco's asset base is
  weighted toward Jorf Lasfar coal. "100% renewable supply" for a
  24/7 flat load is a PPA-accounting statement, not an engineering
  statement, unless paired with either dedicated new-build RES +
  storage or interconnection-balanced imports. The announcement is
  silent on the structure.
- **Grid impact under-disclosed.** A 500 MW flat load is ~7% of
  Morocco's peak demand added over 18–24 months at a single node.
  ONEE's published network plan to 2030 (PDITE) does not appear to
  earmark reinforcements at Nouaceur of this magnitude. Either the
  project is grid-islanded (it isn't — TAQA supplies), or the grid
  plan needs amending. Silence on this is the real story.

## The Morocco-specific hook
Every country with hyperscaler ambitions is dealing with the same
tension — AI load is flat, renewable supply is variable, the
traditional grid assumes industrial consumers that ramp. Morocco is
the first MEA-scale case where this tension is about to become
operational. The OCP industrial base (phosphates, flat load, ~1 GW)
is the incumbent flexibility story; the Nexus site is the same story
retold in a smaller footprint and with foreign capital. The question
is whether ONEE, MASEN and TAQA have a coordination model for a grid
with two flat-load archipelagos (Jorf Lasfar / Safi phosphate corridor
+ Nouaceur compute) both demanding green electrons.
