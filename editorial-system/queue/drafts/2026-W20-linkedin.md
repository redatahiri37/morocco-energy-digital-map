## POST BODY (link-free for algorithm)

In August 2025, ACWA Power won Noor Midelt II at 0.3245 MAD/kWh.

That's ~$32.5/MWh. For 400 MW of solar with a 300 MWh co-located battery. 30-year PPA. No subsidies. No ancillary services market.

For context: the unsubsidized U.S. floor for the same configuration is 4x higher.

Co-located storage in Morocco is now cheaper than the transmission reinforcement it avoids. ONEE just opened a 1.6 GWh standalone BESS tender across ten sites.

The cost case isn't theoretical anymore.

But the Nexus Nouaceur announcement still doesn't mention a battery.
