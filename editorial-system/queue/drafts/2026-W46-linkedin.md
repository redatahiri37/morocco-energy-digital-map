In October 2024, Al Massira, Morocco's second-largest reservoir, was 0.4% full. Marrakech still had water: it is pumped 125 km from that reservoir, through three pumping stations totalling 27 MVA.

Morocco is moving its water security from the sky to the grid.

- The water highways lift water across watersheds. Sebou–Bouregreg runs at 15 m³/s. Phase II to Al Massira is in tender: 210 km, 30 m³/s, three pumping stations.
- Desalination under construction adds 337 million m³ a year. At 3.5 kWh/m³ that is about 180 MW of round-the-clock load, before OCP's own plants.
- CMIP6 puts 2050 rainfall at −10% to −16%. Runoff falls 2–3× faster than that.

ONEE runs both the water and the electricity. The question is whether its grid plan treats those megawatts as fixed load or as flexible demand.
