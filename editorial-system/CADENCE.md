# Weekly Cadence

One Substack post per week. LinkedIn teaser same day. Monthly
synthesis on last Sunday of the month.

## The week
| Day | Who | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon AM | Scout | Scan Tier 1+2 sources | 3–5 signals in raw-signals.md |
| Mon PM | Chief Editor | Pick 1 signal | `[PURSUE]` tag + editorial-log entry |
| Tue | Researcher | Deep dive chosen signal | briefing in queue/briefings/ |
| Wed | Chief Editor → Reda | Email: proposed angle | Reda replies yes / sharpen / kill |
| Thu | Writer | Draft Substack + LinkedIn | two files in queue/drafts/ |
| Fri | Chief Editor → Reda | Draft for review | Reda approves or requests one revision |
| Sat | Reda | Final edit, chart, publish | move files to published/ |
| Sun | — | Read, rest | — |

## The month
Last Sunday: Chief Editor proposes a synthesis brief from the month's
4 Substack posts. Reda writes the synthesis himself (not delegated —
this is where the thesis evolves).

## Escape valves
- Weak Scout week → skip. Republish an underrated past piece or
  write a short "week in charts" instead. Consistency > forced output.
- Reda traveling / no bandwidth → Chief Editor pauses cycle, does
  NOT auto-publish. Email "cycle paused, resume date?"
- Signal appears mid-week that's too hot to wait → Chief Editor
  proposes replacing current draft. Reda decides.

## Why weekly, not bi-daily
Substack rewards rhythm + depth, not frequency. Bi-daily Scout
scans produce noise for a weekly publication. If cadence ever
moves to 2×/week, revisit this file.
