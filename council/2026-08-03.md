### Ruling

SHIP:    OBJ-frontend-engineer-1 — the layer toggles are the map's primary
         interaction (choosing which infrastructure to look at), and they
         are, right now, provably unreachable by keyboard or screen reader
         on the live `docs/` pipeline (`display:none` input, no `tabindex`
         anywhere). That is rung 3 on the precedence ladder — "the audience
         cannot make a decision it should be able to make" — not rung 5
         polish. It is S-sized, reversible, touches no data/schema, and the
         correct pattern already exists in the repo (commit `2ed6340`),
         just shipped to a file no visitor loads. No stronger rung-1/2
         candidate was found this sitting: the closest, OBJ-map-debugger-1

         blocked decision — it is the strongest item held for next sitting.

         read-only investigative pass, not executed within this sitting.
