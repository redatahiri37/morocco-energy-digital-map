Execution of OBJ-map-debugger-1 was **held, not dispatched**, on review of
the Chair's ruling. The ruling is correct that the mailto target is dead
(`example.com` is IANA-reserved, RFC 2606) and that this is the
highest-ranked shippable objective. But the Chair's implementation spec
went further than the objective's own `evidence:` line required — it
named a specific replacement address, `reda.tahiri1@gmail.com`, asserting
it was "confirmed as the site owner's real, monitored mailbox." That
address does not appear anywhere else in this repository; the claim was
an inference, not a confirmed fact, and its similarity to the "reda.tahiri"
placeholder pattern is not evidence of anything.

Publishing a real person's personal email address as a public, site-wide
contact target is a disclosure decision, not a string substitution. It is
hard to reverse once indexed/cached, and it is not a decision an
unattended sitting should make on inference alone — it needs the site
owner's explicit choice of what address (or contact mechanism) to
publish. This is the same spirit as COUNCIL.md's bar on the Chair
handling credentials, extended to a different kind of personal
disclosure.
