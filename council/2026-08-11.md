         **OBJ-coord-validator-2's finding is the most consequential thing
         this sitting surfaced.** Sample of 25 features (20 random + 5
         extremes) across `national-hv.geojson` (947, cites ONEE) and
         `transmission-lines.geojson` (541, cites World Bank Group): 23
         PASS / 2 WARN / 0 FAIL at the individual-coordinate level (no
         gross positioning errors found). But an exhaustive cross-file
         signature match found 98.7% of transmission-lines' features
         share near-identical geometry with national-hv features, and
         71.9% the reverse, at a near-constant ~0.30 km offset across
         matched vertices — a pattern the report states is "inconsistent
         with two genuinely independent sources... consistent with one
         file being mechanically derived from the other... with cosmetic
         property schemas invented afterward to look ONEE-sourced vs.
         WBG-sourced." Recommendation: withdraw or caveat the citations,
         do not wire these files into the live map as currently labeled.
         This closes OBJ-coord-validator-2 and directly produced the new
         OBJ-coord-validator-4 (filed above) and hardened
         OBJ-map-debugger-2's block from "waiting on verification" to
         "blocked on a confirmed citation problem."
